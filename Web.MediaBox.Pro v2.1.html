Web.MediaBox.Pro v2

Developed and created by randybondoc

I worked hard developing this app to address real-world limitations and problems for people trying to multitask with limited capabilities and functions from other 3rd party apps, particularly floating view over anything, and the dedicated audio control that doesn't affect the system or other apps playing. It's built to run free of OS restrictions since no installation is needed, and to play various media formats, including IPTV.

App Features & Info

  • Universal: Works on modern phones, tablets, PCs, Macs, Chromebooks and other devices with a modern browser.
  • Zero Installation: No app installation. No setup. No sign-up. Just open the link and use it instantly.
  • Floating / PiP Video: Keep watching while working, browsing, coding, chatting or doing anything else.
  • Independent Audio Control: Control, mute or adjust the player's audio without taking over your device's entire audio experience.
  • URL & IPTV / HLS Playback: Play compatible videos and streams directly from a URL, including HLS .m3u8 streams commonly used for IPTV.
  • Local Video Playback: Play videos directly from your device — including multiple files and playlists.
  • Client-Side Privacy: Your local videos stay on your device. No unnecessary uploading or account required.
